# Retail Store Sales Trend Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail store sales data using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify monthly sales trends, understand customer demographics, analyze order statuses, and determine the top-performing states.

## Objectives
- Clean and prepare raw retail data for analysis.
- Analyze monthly sales revenue and order volume.
- Compare sales contribution by gender.
- Examine order status distribution.
- Identify the top five states by sales.
- Analyze order counts across customer age groups.
- Enable interactive analysis using dashboard slicers.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Removed duplicate records.
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Standardized and transformed data into an analysis-ready format.
- Prepared relevant fields for sales and customer analysis.

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
A Combo Chart comparing the sum of sales and count of orders by month.

### 2. Gender-Based Sales Contribution
A visualization showing the percentage contribution of male and female customers to total sales.

### 3. Order Status Analysis
Analyzed order counts across four statuses:
- Cancelled
- Delivered
- Refunded
- Returned

### 4. Top 5 States by Sales
Identified the five highest-performing states based on total sales revenue.

### 5. Order Count by Age Group
Grouped customers into age ranges and compared order counts across those groups.

## Interactive Features
The dashboard includes slicers for:
- Month
- Sales Channel
- Category

These filters allow users to explore sales trends and customer patterns dynamically.

## Key Learning Outcomes
This project provided practical experience in preparing raw data, creating interactive dashboards, analyzing customer demographics, and presenting sales performance indicators in a business-friendly format.

Key business insights

1. Gender-Based Sales Analysis
Female customers contributed a higher share of total sales than male customers, indicating stronger sales contribution from women in this dataset. The business could explore product preferences and purchasing patterns by gender to improve targeted marketing.




2. Top-Performing States
Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Karnataka were the top three states by sales. These states could be prioritized for targeted campaigns, customer engagement, and regional sales strategies.




3. Customer Age Group Analysis
Adult customers accounted for approximately 49% of total orders, making this the largest contributing age group. The business could focus on understanding this segment's preferences and tailoring offers to improve engagement and repeat purchases.




4. Sales Channel Analysis
Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart were the leading sales channels in terms of contribution. The business could evaluate each channel's revenue, order volume, and profitability to optimize marketing spend and channel strategy.




5. Monthly Sales and Order Trends
The monthly Combo Chart helps compare sales revenue with order volume, making it easier to identify high-performing months and changes in purchasing activity. Months with high order counts but lower sales may indicate lower average order values.




6. Order Status Analysis
Tracking delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders helps assess order fulfilment and potential revenue leakage. A deeper review of cancellation and return rates could help identify operational issues and opportunities to improve customer experience.

## Conclusion
The dashboard provides an interactive view of retail sales performance, order trends, customer demographics, and state-level sales results. It demonstrates how Excel can be used to turn raw retail data into actionable business insights.
